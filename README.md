## Projects Overview

### [Project 1: ER Wait Time Optimization](./Project_1_ER_Wait_Time)
**Tools:** Excel · [Live dashboard](https://anthonycid-data-insights.lovable.app/project/er-wait-time-optimization)

**Focus:** Throughput optimization and staffing insight by urgency, time block, and weekday.

**Example finding:** Monday visits averaged **101.6 minutes** of total wait time, and evening visits averaged **99.7 minutes**, highlighting where staffing review could be most useful.

### [Project 2: Medical Claims Denial Analysis](./Project_2_Claims_Denial)
**Tools:** Excel, SQL · [Live dashboard](https://anthonycid-data-insights.lovable.app/project/medical-claims-denial-analysis)

**Focus:** Denial patterns by payer, procedure, denial reason, and likely front-end quality drivers.

**Example finding:** The 1,000-claim sample included **328 denied claims** and **$32,138** in denied dollars, with preventable front-end issues such as authorization and billing information appearing in the top reason codes.

### [Project 3: Patient Admissions & Billing Insights](./Project_3_Admissions_Readmissions)
**Tools:** SQL · [Live dashboard](https://anthonycid-data-insights.lovable.app/project/patient-admission-readmission-insights)

**Focus:** Admission volume, condition mix, payer mix, and billing trends to support operational planning.

**Example finding:** The populated admission records show **$711.0M** in total billing, with Blue Cross representing the largest payer share at **$145.3M**.

### [Project 4: Diabetes Indicators & Physical Activity](./Project_4_Diabetes_Indicators)
**Tools:** BigQuery SQL · [Live dashboard](https://anthonycid-data-insights.lovable.app/project/diabetes-indicators-physical-activity)

**Focus:** Behavioral and demographic drivers associated with diabetes risk using a cleaned BRFSS 2015 dataset.

**Example finding:** Diabetes prevalence was **62.2%** among respondents reporting no physical activity versus **44.8%** among physically active respondents in this cleaned sample.

### [Project 5: Heart Disease Risk Analysis](./Project_5_Heart_Disease_Risk)
**Tools:** R, Excel · [Live dashboard](https://anthonycid-data-insights.lovable.app/project/heart-disease-risk-analysis)

**Focus:** End-to-end risk factor analysis and visualization workflow.

**Example finding:** Patients with heart disease had a lower average maximum heart rate (**139.3**) than patients without heart disease (**158.4**) in the 303-record sample.
